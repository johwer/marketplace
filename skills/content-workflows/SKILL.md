## Content Strategy

### Repo Brand Voice
- **Tone**: Professional, empathetic, data-driven, solution-oriented
- **Audience**: HR managers, CFOs, company leadership in Swedish/Nordic companies
- **Language**: Clear and accessible — avoid overly technical jargon
- **Languages**: sv (primary), en, da, no, fi

### Content Pillars
1. **ServiceA Management** — Best practices, legal compliance, process optimization
2. **Workplace Health** — Prevention, rehabilitation, organizational health
3. **Data & Insights** — ServiceE, benchmarking, trend analysis
4. **Customer Success** — Case studies, testimonials, ROI stories
5. **Industry Updates** — Labor law changes, research findings, market trends

## Blog Post Template

```markdown
# [Headline — clear, keyword-rich, under 70 chars]

**Meta description:** [150-160 chars summary for SEO]

## Introduction
[Hook with a surprising statistic or common pain point]
[State the problem]
[Preview the solution]

## [Section 1 — The Problem]
[Data-backed description of the challenge]

## [Section 2 — The Solution]
[Actionable advice with Repo context]

## [Section 3 — Results/Proof]
[Customer examples, service-e, benchmarks]

## Sammanfattning
[Key takeaways as bullet points]
[CTA — demo, whitepaper, contact]
```

## Email Campaign Structure

### Welcome/Nurture Sequence
1. Welcome + brand story (Day 0)
2. Top challenge in their industry (Day 3)
3. Case study from similar company (Day 7)
4. Free resource / whitepaper (Day 14)
5. Demo/meeting CTA (Day 21)

### Product Update Email
- Subject: [Feature name] — [benefit in 5 words]
- Lead: What changed and why they should care
- Body: 2-3 key benefits with visuals
- CTA: Try it now / Learn more
